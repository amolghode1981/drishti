Database has 3 readings for the 13 images. Readings taken for ViT-B/32, ViT-B/16 and ViT-B/14.
Upon running permutation test, results are not in agreement with ratio based results.
Following this intuitionally, it means that if the p-value is low, it takes many permutation turns to generate the ratios
that would falsly treated as musical. In other words:
If the label grouping were meaningless — just random — you would expect random shuffles to beat you roughly 500 out of 1000 times (50%).
If x out of 1000 beat you. It would reflect on the whether the grouping is genuinely capturing something real. It is not luck.

If the grouping is capturing some truth about the image, p < 0.05 would mean that less than 5% of random shuffles only result in value
that is wrongly considered as musical while it is non-musical.
